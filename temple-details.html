Kashi Vishwanath Temple

A revered Jyotirlinga temple that embodies the eternal energy of Lord Shiva.

Temple Overview

Kashi Vishwanath stands on the ghats of the Ganga and is one of the most powerful pilgrimage sites for Shaivite devotees. The temple radiates a spiritual aura of transformation and liberation.

  • Location: Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh
  • Deity: Lord Shiva
  • Best Time: Maha Shivaratri
Back to Temples

Seva & Darshan

Morning mangal aarti, mid-day bhog, and evening sandhya aarti are the three principal ceremonies.
