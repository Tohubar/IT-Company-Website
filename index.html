About Sanatan Dharma

Sanatan Dharma is a living tradition rooted in harmony, devotion, and self-realization.

Philosophy

Guided by the Vedas and Upanishads, the path emphasizes dharma, karma, and moksha through selfless living.

Practices

Daily sadhana, mantra chanting, and mindful rituals cultivate inner peace and spiritual awakening.

Culture

Festivals, pilgrimages, and sacred arts celebrate devotion, community, and reverence for the divine.

Deities

Pooja & Rituals

Step-by-step guidance for sacred rituals and ceremonies.

Aarti

  1. Light the diya and offer incense.
  2. Chant the aarti mantra in devotion.
  3. Circle the lamp clockwise with gratitude.

Navratri Pooja

  1. Cleanse the altar with holy water.
  2. Offer flowers, kumkum, and prasad.
  3. Recite Durga Saptashati verses.

Ganesh Chaturthi

  1. Invoke Ganesh with modak offerings.
  2. Sing mantras and perform aarti.
  3. Immerse the idol with prayers.

Mandirs

Experience sacred temples and their timeless heritage.

Kashi Vishwanath

Revered as the jyotirlinga in Varanasi, symbolizing moksha and Shiva devotion.

  • Timings: 4:00 AM - 11:00 PM
  • Festivals: Mahashivratri, Dev Deepawali
  • Rituals: Rudrabhishek, Ganga Aarti

Somnath

First of the twelve jyotirlingas, celebrated for resilience and devotion.

  • Timings: 6:00 AM - 9:00 PM
  • Festivals: Kartik Purnima, Shravan
  • Rituals: Aarti, Sandhya Darshan

Vaishno Devi

Journey to the Trikuta hills for blessings of the Divine Mother.

  • Timings: 5:00 AM - 10:00 PM
  • Festivals: Navratri, Diwali
  • Rituals: Cave darshan, Bhajan

Tirupati Balaji

One of the most visited temples, offering darshan of Lord Venkateswara.

  • Timings: 2:30 AM - 1:00 AM
  • Festivals: Brahmotsavam
  • Rituals: Suprabhata Seva, Kalyanotsavam

Pilgrimages

Plan sacred journeys with guidance, travel tips, and testimonials.

Kedarnath

Best Time: May - October

How to Reach: Trek from Gaurikund or helicopter services.

Stay: Ashrams, guest houses, and Himalayan camps.

Rishikesh

Best Time: February - April

How to Reach: Rail to Haridwar, road to Rishikesh.

Stay: Yoga retreats and riverside cottages.

Haridwar

Best Time: October - March

How to Reach: Direct trains and highways.

Stay: Heritage hotels and dharamshalas.

Tirupati

Best Time: September - February

How to Reach: Tirupati airport and railhead.

Stay: Temple guest houses and pre-booked tours.
