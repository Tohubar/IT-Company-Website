Aarti - Light Offering

Illuminate the diya, offer flowers, and chant with rhythmic bells.

  1. 1. Prepare the diya and incense in a sacred plate.
  2. 2. Circle the lamp clockwise in front of the deity.
  3. 3. Ring the bell and chant Om as petals fall.
  4. 4. Offer the light to the devotees as prasad.
