About Sanatan Dharma

Sanatan Dharma is the eternal path that guides seekers toward harmony, self-realization, and compassion.

Rooted in ancient wisdom, Sanatan Dharma embraces diverse paths to truth. Its teachings explore the four Purusharthas—Dharma (righteousness), Artha (prosperity), Kama (fulfillment), and Moksha (liberation)—helping individuals live with balance and purpose.

Its philosophy encourages mindfulness, service, and devotion, with scriptures like the Vedas, Upanishads, and Bhagavad Gita serving as guiding lights. The tradition honors the unity of all existence through reverence for nature, rituals, and meditation.
