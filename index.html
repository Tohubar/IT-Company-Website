About Sanatan Dharma

The eternal philosophy rooted in cosmic order, compassion, and self-realization.

Philosophy & History

Sanatan Dharma is the timeless way of life emphasizing righteous living, compassion, and the pursuit of spiritual liberation. Its teachings span millennia, nurturing harmony between the self, society, and the cosmos.

“Truth is one, the wise call it by many names.” — Rig Veda

Core Principles

  • Dharma: The sacred duty aligned with truth.
  • Karma: The law of cause and effect.
  • Reincarnation: The soul’s journey through lifetimes.
  • Moksha: Liberation through spiritual realization.

Universal Appeal

Sanatan Dharma honors diversity of paths, nurturing unity through meditation, devotion, knowledge, and service. It embraces every seeker on the path of eternal truth.

Divine Deities

Explore the manifestations of the Divine with stories, symbols, and sacred chants.

Lord Shiva

Embodiment of transformation and stillness, Shiva guides seekers to inner peace.

Symbols: Trishul, Damru, Crescent Moon

Lord Vishnu

Guardian of cosmic order, Vishnu sustains the universe with compassion.

Symbols: Conch, Lotus, Sudarshan Chakra

Goddess Durga

Divine mother of courage and strength, she removes obstacles from the path.

Symbols: Lion, Sword, Lotus

Lord Ganesha

Beloved remover of obstacles and patron of wisdom, art, and beginnings.

Symbols: Modak, Mooshika, Om
