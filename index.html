Pooja & Ritual Guides

Experience the sacred rituals with interactive steps, mantra audio, and spiritual reflections.

Sandhya Aarti

Daily Ritual
  1. Cleanse the space and light the diya.
  2. Offer flowers, incense, and sacred water.
  3. Chant the Aarti mantra with a calm breath.
  4. Offer gratitude and distribute prasad.
"Let the lamp of wisdom dispel the darkness of ignorance."
Book Aarti

Navratri Pooja

Festival Ritual
  1. Prepare a sanctified altar with kalash and grains.
  2. Invoke the Devi with sacred chants.
  3. Offer nine forms of devotion with flowers and fruits.
  4. Conclude with kirtan and communal blessings.
"The Divine Mother awakens strength, courage, and compassion."
Book Navratri

Ganesh Chaturthi

Community Pooja
  1. Install the murti with reverence and purity.
  2. Offer modak, durva grass, and incense.
  3. Chant Ganapati Atharvashirsha.
  4. Conclude with visarjan and prayer.
"May the remover of obstacles bless every beginning."

Mandirs & Sacred Temples

Discover the spiritual sanctuaries of Sanatan Dharma with timings, rituals, and virtual glimpses.

Kashi Vishwanath

Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh • Timings: 3:00 AM - 11:00 PM • Rituals: Mangala Aarti, Rudrabhishek.

Festival: Mahashivratri • Significance: Jyotirlinga of Shiva

Somnath

Veraval, Gujarat • Timings: 6:00 AM - 9:00 PM • Rituals: Abhishekam, Evening Aarti.

Festival: Kartik Purnima • Significance: First Jyotirlinga

Vaishno Devi

Katra, Jammu • Timings: Open 24 hrs • Rituals: Bhajan, Mata Aarti.

Festival: Navratri • Significance: Holy cave shrine

Tirupati Balaji

Tirumala, Andhra Pradesh • Timings: 2:30 AM - 1:00 AM • Rituals: Suprabhatam, Kalyanotsavam.

Festival: Brahmotsavam • Significance: Lord Venkateswara

Golden Temple

Amritsar, Punjab • Timings: 4:00 AM - 11:00 PM • Rituals: Kirtan, Langar seva.

Festival: Guru Nanak Jayanti • Significance: Sacred sarovar
