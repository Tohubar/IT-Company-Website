About the CSE Department

Mission & Vision

We foster inclusive, ethical, and impactful computing solutions. Our vision is to lead the region in transformative research and graduate future-ready engineers.

  • Student-centered, research-driven learning environment.
  • Strong partnerships with industry and academic institutions.

Academic Programs

Structured pathways for undergraduate and graduate learners with flexible specializations.

B.S. in Computer Science & Engineering

  • Duration: 4 years
  • Course Structure: Core computing, engineering math, systems, electives.
  • Admission Criteria: High school STEM prerequisites.
  • Career Paths: Software engineer, systems analyst, product developer.
Apply Now

M.S. in Computer Science

  • Duration: 2 years
  • Course Structure: Advanced AI, data systems, capstone research.
  • Admission Criteria: STEM bachelor’s degree + GRE (optional).
  • Career Paths: Data scientist, ML engineer, researcher.
Apply Now

Ph.D. in Computer Engineering

  • Duration: 4-5 years
  • Course Structure: Research seminars, dissertation, teaching practicum.
  • Admission Criteria: Master’s degree + research proposal.
  • Career Paths: Faculty, R&D scientist, innovation leader.
Apply Now
