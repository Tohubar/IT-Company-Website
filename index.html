Rituals & Pooja

Step-by-step Sacred Practices

Ganesh Chaturthi Pooja

  1. Purify the space with incense and diya.
  2. Offer durva grass, modak, and flowers.
  3. Chant the Ganapati mantra with devotion.

Navratri Pooja

  1. Establish a sacred kalash and red cloth altar.
  2. Offer kumkum, rice, and seasonal fruits.
  3. Invoke the Divine Mother with aarti.

Diwali Pooja

  1. Illuminate the home with rows of diyas.
  2. Offer sweets and lotus flowers to Lakshmi.
  3. Recite Sri Sukta or Lakshmi mantra.

Temples & Pilgrimages

Sacred Journeys Across Bharat

Kashi Vishwanath

Experience the holy ghats of Varanasi with nightly aarti rituals on the Ganga.

  • Significance: Jyotirlinga of Lord Shiva.
  • Festivals: Shivaratri, Dev Deepawali.
  • Timings: 4:00 AM - 11:00 PM.
Somnath

Discover the first Jyotirlinga with waves of devotion along the Arabian Sea.

  • Significance: Eternal shrine of Shiva.
  • Festivals: Kartik Purnima, Shravan.
  • Timings: 6:00 AM - 9:00 PM.
Vaishno Devi

Embark on a mountain pilgrimage to the sacred cave shrine of Mata Rani.

  • Significance: Divine Mother manifestation.
  • Festivals: Navratri, Diwali.
  • Timings: Open year-round.
Tirupati Balaji

Witness sacred darshan of Lord Venkateshwara with grand daily seva rituals.

  • Significance: Vishnu temple of prosperity.
  • Festivals: Brahmotsavam.
  • Timings: 3:00 AM - 11:00 PM.
