Lakshmi Puja

Wealth & Prosperity
  • Flowers & garlands
  • Diya & camphor
  • Rice, turmeric, kumkum
  1. Purify the space and light the diya.
  2. Offer flowers and chant the Lakshmi mantra.
  3. Perform aarti and distribute prasad.

Satyanarayana Puja

Gratitude & Harmony
  • Kalash with sacred water
  • Tulsi, betel leaves
  • Fruits, sweets, coconut
  1. Invoke Lord Vishnu with sankalpa.
  2. Read the vrat katha and offer naivedya.
  3. Conclude with aarti and blessings.

Best Timing: Purnima morning

Durga Navaratri

Shakti & Protection
  • Devi yantra & lamps
  • Red flowers & chunri
  • Dhol & devotional songs
  1. Set up the altar with a kalash.
  2. Perform daily aarti and chant Durga Saptashati.
  3. Offer bhog and conduct kumari puja.
