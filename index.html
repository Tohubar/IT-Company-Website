Puja Details

Guided rituals with materials, steps, and mantras

Required Materials

  • 🪔 Diya
  • 🌺 Flowers
  • 🧴 Incense
  • 🥥 Coconut
  • 📿 Mala
  • 💧 Holy water

Step-by-step Puja

  1. Cleanse the space with incense and light the diya.
  2. Offer flowers and water while chanting the invocation.
  3. Recite the main mantra with a mala for 108 counts.
  4. Conclude with aarti and distribute prasad.

Mantras & Downloads
