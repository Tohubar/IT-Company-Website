Rituals & Pooja Guides

Step-by-step guides enhanced with 3D animations and audio recitations.

Aarti Ceremony

  1. 1. Light the diya and center your breath.
  2. 2. Offer flowers with gratitude.
  3. 3. Rotate the flame clockwise 7 times.
  4. 4. Chant the aarti mantra together.
3D Diya Animation

Ganesh Chaturthi

  1. 1. Prepare the altar with modaks.
  2. 2. Offer sacred water and sandalwood.
  3. 3. Recite Ganapati Atharvashirsha.
  4. 4. Conclude with prasad sharing.
3D Modak Display

Navratri Pooja

  1. 1. Decorate with kalash and mango leaves.
  2. 2. Invoke the nine forms of Devi.
  3. 3. Sing devotional garba songs.
  4. 4. Light 108 lamps together.
3D Kalash Animation

Diwali Pooja

  1. 1. Cleanse space with incense.
  2. 2. Offer lotus flowers to Lakshmi.
  3. 3. Recite Lakshmi stotram.
  4. 4. Distribute sweets with family.
3D Rangoli Animation
