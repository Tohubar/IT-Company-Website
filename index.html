FAQ

+

How do students start learning?

Create an account, pick a pathway, and start with the onboarding quiz. We build a personalized dashboard with recommended lessons and mini challenges.

+

Are courses beginner-friendly?

Yes! Every course includes guided walkthroughs, practice quizzes, and mentor tips for students who are just getting started.

+

Can students track progress?

Absolutely. The dashboard shows lesson completion, streaks, badges, and upcoming tasks at a glance.

+

Do students get certificates?

Every completed course unlocks a digital certificate and shareable badge for profiles and resumes.

+

Is there support for teachers?

Yes. Educators can access classroom dashboards, progress reports, and curated lesson plans.
