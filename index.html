Pooja (Rituals)

Step-by-step guidance, immersive animations, and devotional resources for daily and festive rituals.

Ritual Flow

  1. Prepare the altar with flowers, kalash, and sacred cloth.
  2. Light the diya and invoke auspiciousness.
  3. Offer water, sandalwood, and fragrant incense.
  4. Chant mantras and perform Aarti with devotion.
  5. Distribute prasad and conclude with gratitude.

Mandirs (Temples)

Explore the history, rituals, and divine presence of iconic temples across Bharat.

Kashi Vishwanath

History & Significance: One of the twelve Jyotirlingas, nestled on the ghats of Varanasi.

  • Timings: 4:00 AM - 9:00 PM
  • Festivals: Mahashivratri, Dev Deepawali
  • Rituals: Rudrabhishek, Mangala Aarti
Virtual Tour

Somnath

History & Significance: The first Jyotirlinga, symbol of resilience and faith.

  • Timings: 6:00 AM - 9:30 PM
  • Festivals: Kartik Purnima, Shravan
  • Rituals: Abhishek, Sandhya Aarti
Travel Guide

Vaishno Devi

History & Significance: A sacred mountain cave shrine revered for divine blessings.

  • Timings: Open 24/7
  • Festivals: Navratri, Shravan Mela
  • Rituals: Charan Pooja, Darshan

Pilgrimages

Plan sacred routes with guidance on travel, seasons, rituals, and accommodations.

Kedarnath

Best time: May - June & Sept - Oct. Trek and helicopter options from Gaurikund.

  • Stay: Dharamshalas & eco hotels
  • Rituals: Abhishek, evening Aarti
  • Guide: 2-day mountain itinerary

Rishikesh & Haridwar

Best time: Feb - April & Oct - Nov. Ideal for yoga, Ganga aarti, and retreats.

  • Stay: River camps & ashrams
  • Rituals: Ganga Aarti, Havan
  • Guide: 3-day wellness itinerary

Ayodhya

Best time: Oct - March. Explore Ram Mandir and historical ghats.

  • Stay: Heritage stays & guest houses
  • Rituals: Ram katha, Janmotsav
  • Guide: 2-day heritage itinerary

Mahabharata
