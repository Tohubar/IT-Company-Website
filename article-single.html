The Five Elements in Daily Worship

Rituals · 10 June 2024 · 8 minute read

The pancha tattva — earth, water, fire, air, and space — gently remind us that devotion is both grounded and expansive. When we honor the elements, we are honoring the rhythms that sustain life.

“When the flame steadies, the mind follows. When the breath softens, the heart opens.”

Earth is represented by the altar and the offerings. Water is poured with mindful reverence. Fire is the sacred flame, inviting clarity. Air is the incense and the breath. Space is the silence between each mantra.

As you arrange your puja space, pause with each element. Let your practice be as balanced as the universe itself.

“Let every breath be a remembrance, and every action an offering.”
